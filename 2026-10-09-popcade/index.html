Popcade

2026-10-09 · all games

Tap a tray cube: it flies up its lane and bursts into balls that pop every cube of their own colour they touch, in chains. 100 levels, boosters, lessons, a shop, a season board, lives and streaks.

Web (three.js)

Play in the browser

Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox on a desktop or laptop (about 24 MB on the first load). Downloaded instead: double-click Play offline (Windows).bat or Play offline (Mac).command in the web folder — a browser does not run the game straight from index.html.

Windows

Download Popcade-Windows.zip

  1. Extract the zip (right-click → Extract All…).
  2. Double-click Popcade.exe in the Popcade folder (the whole folder is needed).
  3. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”: More info → Run anyway.

Mac (Apple silicon and Intel, macOS 12 or later)

Download Popcade-Mac.zip

  1. Double-click the zip: a Popcade-Mac folder appears.
  2. Double-click Run perf test.command first: it prepares the app for this Mac and measures the frame rate (it puts popcade-perf-mac.zip on the Desktop).
  3. Then double-click Popcade.app to play. Details: READ ME FIRST.txt in the folder.
