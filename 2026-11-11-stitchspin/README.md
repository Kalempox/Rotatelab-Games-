# STITCHBLAST (2026-11-11)

Tap a bobbin: it rides the loop and pulls every cube of its own colour that it can reach off the sculpture. Turn the sculpture with a finger to bring more cubes into reach, and clear it before the rack is full. 15 levels, each its own figure, with lessons, boosters, lives and coins. An early build: the Unity players still lack a few screens the browser game has.

- **web** — [play in the browser](web/) (three.js)
- **windows** — [STITCHBLAST-Windows.zip](windows/STITCHBLAST-Windows.zip) (Unity)
- **mac** — [STITCHBLAST-Mac.zip](mac/STITCHBLAST-Mac.zip) (Unity)
