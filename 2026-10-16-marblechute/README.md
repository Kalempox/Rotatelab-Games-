# Marblechute (2026-10-16)

Tap a ready pop-it tile: its nine marbles spill down the funnel onto the belt, and each crate takes three of its colour. Ship every crate before the belt is full. 15 levels, with three boosters, lessons, lives and coins.

- **web** — [play in the browser](web/) (three.js)
- **windows** — [Marblechute-Windows.zip](windows/Marblechute-Windows.zip) (Unity)
- **mac** — [Marblechute-Mac.zip](mac/Marblechute-Mac.zip) (Unity)
